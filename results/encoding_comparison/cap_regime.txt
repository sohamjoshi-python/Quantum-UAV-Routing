CAP-REGIME ESTIMATE (is cap_per_request binding?)
============================================================
Interpretation: implied mean k_r is estimated from pairwise couplers assuming ~uniform k_r. If implied k_r >> cap, the cap binds and the coupler ratio understates the true (uncapped) pairwise disadvantage.
